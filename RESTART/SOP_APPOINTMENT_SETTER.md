# SOP — Appointment Setter

**Role:** Appointment Setter (independent contractor)
**Reports to:** Oliver Carroll, OliverSEO
**Runs alongside:** Sales Closer (separate person, separate SOP — see `SOP_SALES_CLOSER.md`)

The setter does NOT sell. The setter's only job is to turn cold prospects into booked appointments on the closer's calendar.

---

## 1. The job in one sentence

Contact small businesses that miss calls or have bad/no websites, and book 15-minute appointments with the Sales Closer.

## 2. What they sell appointments FOR

| Offer | Price | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| AI Receptionist | $1,700/mo (3,000 min included) | Businesses missing after-hours calls: insurance, law, medical, HVAC/plumbing, real estate, home services |
| Website design | $997 / $1,997 / $4,997 packages | Wyoming small businesses with outdated or no website |

**The demo line is the setter's best tool:** (307) 464-9517 — "Claire." Telling a prospect to call it and experience the AI themselves books more appointments than any pitch.

## 3. Daily activity requirements (non-negotiable)

This is the fix for what went wrong last time: a commission-only rep went silent for weeks and produced nothing, with zero visibility. Activity is now tracked daily, not trusted on faith.

- **Minimum 25 outreach touches per working day** (calls, emails, DMs, walk-ins — any mix)
- **Log every touch the same day** in the shared Google Sheet: business name, contact, channel, outcome, next step
- **Daily end-of-day check-in** (text or Slack, 2 minutes): touches made, conversations had, appointments booked
- **Miss 2 check-ins in a week without notice = exit conversation.** Commission-only does not mean accountability-free.

## 4. Scripts

### Cold call opener
> "Hey, this is [Name] with OliverSEO out of Gillette. Quick question — when someone calls your office after 5, what happens to that call? ... That's exactly why I'm calling. We run an AI receptionist that answers every after-hours call, takes the caller's info, and emails it to you in real time. Don't take my word for it — call (307) 464-9517 right now and talk to it yourself. Then let me get you 15 minutes with Oliver's closer to see what it'd look like on your line. Does Tuesday or Thursday work better?"

### Cold email / DM
> Subject: Your phone after 5pm
>
> Hi [FirstName] — when a customer calls [Business] after hours, do they get voicemail? Most hang up and call the next listing.
>
> We run an AI receptionist that answers every call 24/7, takes the lead's info, and emails it to you instantly. Call our demo line and talk to it yourself: (307) 464-9517.
>
> Worth 15 minutes to see it on your own number? Here's the calendar: [booking link]

### The only goal
Every conversation ends in one of three outcomes, logged in the sheet:
1. **Appointment booked** (date/time on closer's calendar)
2. **Follow-up scheduled** (specific date, in the sheet)
3. **Dead** (marked dead with reason — no zombie leads)

## 5. Qualification checklist (before booking)

Only book appointments that pass ALL of these:
- [ ] Decision-maker will be on the call (owner or manager who can say yes)
- [ ] Business actually misses calls OR has a website problem (they said so)
- [ ] They know the meeting is 15 minutes and roughly what it's about
- [ ] Phone number and email confirmed

Unqualified appointments don't count for pay.

## 6. Pay

- **$40 per qualified appointment that shows up** (held, decision-maker present)
- **$100 bonus per appointment that becomes a paying client**
- Paid weekly via Square/Venmo for the prior week's held appointments
- No commission on the deal itself — that's the closer's. No overlap, no disputes.

At 3 held appointments/week the setter makes ~$480–900/mo for part-time work; a client closed from their appointments pays Oliver $1,700/mo recurring, so the math works at even a 15% close rate.

## 7. Handoff to closer

When an appointment is booked:
1. Add it to the closer's calendar (Calendly or shared Google Calendar)
2. Fill the handoff row in the sheet: business, contact name, what they complained about, which offer fits (AI receptionist / website / both), any objections already raised
3. Text the closer same-day: "New appt: [Business], [day/time], wants [offer], pain point: [X]"

## 8. Weekly review (every Friday, 15 min with Oliver)

- Touches, conversations, appointments: actuals vs. minimums
- Which script/channel is producing — double down on it
- Kill what produced nothing for 2 straight weeks

## 9. Agreement terms (summary)

- Independent contractor, at-will, either party can end it any time with written notice
- Pay only for qualified held appointments logged in the sheet before termination
- No client info, pricing, or strategy shared outside the business
- 2-week trial: minimum 10 held appointments in the first 2 weeks or the arrangement ends — stated upfront at hiring
