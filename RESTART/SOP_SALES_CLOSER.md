# SOP — Sales Closer

**Role:** Sales Closer (independent contractor)
**Reports to:** Oliver Carroll, OliverSEO
**Runs alongside:** Appointment Setter (separate person, separate SOP — see `SOP_APPOINTMENT_SETTER.md`)

The closer does NOT prospect. The closer's only job is to run the appointments the setter books and turn them into signed clients.

---

## 1. The job in one sentence

Take the 15-minute appointments on the calendar, run the demo, handle objections, and close.

| Offer | Price | Closer's commission |

| AI Receptionist | $1,700/mo, month-to-month, 3,000 min included, $0.30/min overage | **30% of month 1** ($510) + **10% of months 2–6** ($170/mo × 5) = up to $1,360/client |

**Commission is paid only after the client's money clears** (first month's payment for AI receptionist; full payment — deposit + final — for websites). Paid within 7 days of funds clearing. If a client refunds within 30 days, the commission on that payment is returned. Same claw-back rules as the original agreements.

## 3. The call structure (15–20 min)

1. **Confirm the pain (2 min).** "The setter mentioned you're missing after-hours calls — walk me through what happens now when someone calls at 7pm."
2. **Live demo (5 min).** Have them call (307) 464-9517 on speaker, right there on the call. Let Claire do the selling. For website deals: pull up their current site and one portfolio piece side by side.
3. **The math (3 min).** One missed call for an insurance agent, lawyer, or HVAC company is worth $500–5,000+. The AI costs $1,700/mo. One saved client a month pays for it; everything after that is profit they're currently losing.
4. **Price, straight (2 min).** "$1,700 a month flat, 3,000 minutes included, month-to-month, cancel any time. No contract trap."
5. **Close (3 min).** "We can have it answering your line this week. Want me to get you set up?"

Full objection handling lives in the sales script and price justification docs — key ones:
- **"It's just a fancy voicemail."** Voicemail loses 80% of callers. This answers, converses, captures the lead, and emails it to you in real time. Call the demo line — would you leave a voicemail a message? You just had a conversation.
- **"Too expensive."** What's one new client worth to you? If it's over $1,700, this pays for itself with one saved call a month.
- **"Let me think about it."** "Totally fair. While you think, every after-hours call this week still goes to voicemail. Want to run it for a month and let the results decide?"

## 4. Rules

- **Never discount.** $1,700 is the price. The referral program ($500 off the next invoice per referral that becomes a paying client) is the only discount mechanism, and it's for existing clients.
- **Month-to-month is the closer's friend** — there's no contract objection to fight.
- **Every appointment outcome is logged same-day** in the shared sheet: closed / follow-up (with date) / dead (with reason).
- **No-shows go back to the setter** for one rebook attempt, then dead.

## 5. Activity requirements

- Run every appointment on the calendar or give 24h notice to rebook
- Log every outcome same-day
- **Weekly Friday review with Oliver** (15 min): close rate, objections heard, what's killing deals
- Expected close rate on qualified appointments: **20%+** after the first 10 appointments. Below that for 3 straight weeks = the exit conversation.

## 6. Handoff to Oliver (fulfillment)

When a deal closes:
1. Collect payment on the call if possible (Square invoice, link sent while still on the phone)
2. Fill the new-client row: business, contact, offer, number to forward, email for lead delivery, go-live expectations
3. Text Oliver immediately — onboarding starts within 24 hours
4. Tell the client about the referral program before hanging up: "$500 off your next invoice for anyone you send us who signs up."

## 7. Agreement terms (summary)

- Independent contractor, at-will, either party can end it any time with written notice
- Commission only on payments that cleared before termination; residuals (months 2–6) continue to be paid post-termination for clients closed while active
- Confidentiality: no client info, pricing, or strategy shared outside the business
- 30-day trial: at least 1 closed client in the first 30 days (given a full appointment calendar) or the arrangement ends — stated upfront at hiring
