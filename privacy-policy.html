Privacy policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Oliver SEO ("we," "us") operates oliverseo.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Information you give us: when you submit our contact form, we receive the details you provide — name, business name, email address, phone number, budget range, and your message.
  • Information collected automatically: we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site (pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type). This uses cookies and collects data in aggregate; it does not identify you personally to us.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide quotes or services you request
  • To communicate with you about projects you've engaged us for
  • To improve our website and services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

Third-Party Services

  • Web3Forms processes our contact form submissions and delivers them to our email.
  • Google Analytics provides anonymous site usage statistics. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and may process technical data (such as IP addresses) to serve and secure the site.
  • Square processes payments for our services. We never see or store your full card details.

Cookies

This site uses cookies only for analytics purposes (Google Analytics). You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work normally.

Data Retention

We keep contact form submissions and client communications as long as needed to serve you and meet legal or accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your Rights

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we'll handle it promptly.

Children's Privacy

Our services are for businesses and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new "last updated" date.
